A note on our numbers, in plain English

Many income projections are built to impress you. They project the best market years forward and make almost any withdrawal look safe. We deliberately do the opposite.

The return assumptions in this calculator sit at or below what major institutional forecasters project for the next 10 to 15 years, and they are net of advisory fees. If the market does better than we assume, your reviews become good news. If we assumed the best and the market delivered average, you would find out years too late.

We would rather under promise and over deliver. That is how we build every plan at Jantz Wealth Solutions, and this calculator is no exception.
